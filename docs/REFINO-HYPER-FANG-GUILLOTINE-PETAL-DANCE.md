# Pixel art refinement: Hyper Fang, Guillotine, Petal Dance

Only bespoke Torre 2 VFX was changed, preserving all 14 approved specific moves and all original Poké Arena 2D moves.

* **Hyper Fang**: two large prominent white outlined rat incisors, spaced apart and moving with the existing upper jaw. Original bite timing/physics unchanged.
* **Guillotine**: two independent large steel/ivory serrated blades, visibly wide apart on windup, snapping shut with a compact pixel impact. Original hit/KO probability unchanged.
* **Petal Dance**: 2px tiles instead of 3px (about 33% smaller); six pink palettes to vary each petal. Original movement, independent collisions and self-confusion unchanged.

All three are still awaiting your visual approval. Phaser/WebGL pixel rectangles only, no Canvas 2D or bitmap substitutes.
