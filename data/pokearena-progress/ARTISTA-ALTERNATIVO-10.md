# Alternate artist sprite release path (Alpha 0.71.3)

The ten new missing native species initially came from SpriteCollab's base directories, where per-animation credits sometimes identified official Chunsoft art as `Unspecified`. This change **replaces only those exact ten already quarantined local PNG sets**, using fan-artist variant folders with current CC BY-NC or PMDCollab contributor credits.

Source directories:
- `0132`: `sprite/0132/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0133`: `sprite/0133/0002` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0480`: `sprite/0480/0000/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0481`: `sprite/0481/0001/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0482`: `sprite/0482/0001/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0196`: `sprite/0196/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0197`: `sprite/0197/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0282`: `sprite/0282/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0286`: `sprite/0286/0000/0001` plus corresponding independent portrait source
- `0128`: `sprite/0128/0002` plus corresponding independent portrait source

The actor-approved candidate is noncommercial and this repo's visual provenance audit still checks every PNG against its exact source and contributors. The producer uses the official PMD manifest for sizes/durations; for unavailable moves it uses explicit mappings and records those approximations. It does **not** claim source authenticity, Nintendo/Pokémon rights, identity with official art or a public release approval. Before release, inspect 30 forms in Chromium, verify battle animations, maintain creator attribution and confirm rights for all incorporated material. The approved baseline and main Pages are never overwritten by this isolated step.
